Meet Coach Tinu

More Than a Gym.
A Family.

"ስፖርት ለሰውነት ብቻ ሳይሆን ለአእምሮም ነው" "Sport is not just for the body, but for the mind too"

Beteseb (ቤተሰብ) means "family" in Amharic — and that's exactly what we are. Coach Tinu is a passionate fitness professional based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with over 11 years of experience transforming lives through sport.

With a community-driven approach that combines physical training with mental wellness, Coach Tinu has built a movement that goes beyond the gym. Through the Five Gates Campaign and the Mind the Mind initiative, he's committed to holistic transformation — body, mind, and spirit.
